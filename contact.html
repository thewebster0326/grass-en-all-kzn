Before you get in touch

A few useful answers.

What should I include in my quote request?

Your location, approximate lawn measurements and a few photos are the most helpful starting point. Also mention whether you need turf only or help with ground preparation and laying.

Can I ask about delivery?

Yes. Send your delivery location and approximate quantity. The team can confirm the available options for your area.

What if I do not know my lawn size?

Use the calculator on our Services page for a rectangular space, or send your approximate dimensions and photos on WhatsApp for help.

Can I email instead of using WhatsApp?

Yes. Email grassenallkzn@gmail.com or call us on 064 547 0866.
