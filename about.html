Who we are

Natural quality.
Local know-how.

Established in 2010, GRASS EN ALL Instant Turf helps customers across KwaZulu-Natal create beautiful, usable green spaces with natural instant lawn.

With branches in Pietermaritzburg and Durban, we bring local knowledge and practical guidance to every enquiry — from a compact home garden to a larger estate or commercial space.

Our approach is simple: understand the space, prepare it properly and help every new lawn get the best start.
