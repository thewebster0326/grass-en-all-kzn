Turf laying FAQs

What to expect.

Can turf be fitted around paths and pool areas?

Yes. Turf can be trimmed around fixed features, stepping stones, beds and boundaries. Share photos so the layout and detail work can be discussed.

Why are the joins staggered?

A staggered, brick-like pattern avoids long continuous seams and helps create a more natural-looking lawn surface.

What should happen immediately after laying?

The turf should make good contact with the soil and be watered thoroughly. Ongoing moisture and suitable aftercare are important while it establishes.
