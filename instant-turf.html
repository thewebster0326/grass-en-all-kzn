Why instant lawn?

A finished garden starts here.

Instant turf is mature grass grown before being lifted and supplied for your space. Once laid on prepared soil, it immediately transforms bare ground into a green lawn.

It is a practical option for home gardens, new builds, estates, schools and business grounds where you want natural grass and a neat result without the long growing period.

Instant turf FAQs

Before you order.

How much turf should I order?

Start with your measured area in square metres and allow a little extra for cuts and shaping. Use our calculator for a rectangular space or contact us about irregular layouts.

Can turf be laid over my existing lawn?

Old growth, weeds and stones should be cleared first. A clean, level soil surface helps the new turf make good contact and establish more effectively.

Does instant turf need immediate watering?

Yes. Water thoroughly as soon as it is laid and keep the new lawn consistently moist during establishment, following the guidance for your conditions.
